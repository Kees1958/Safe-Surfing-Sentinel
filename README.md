# Safe-Surfing-Sentinel
Zero configuration extension which warns users for SCAMS, SNOOPING and SKIMMING

Safe Surfing Sentinel is zero configuration meaning no settings pages full of toggles and settings you don't understand, no decisions to get wrong. Install it, and it quietly gets to work.

WHAT IT DOES

Blocks the bad third parties.

A huge amount of what makes the web slow, creepy, or dangerous isn't the site you're on — it's the trackers and scripts that site is quietly loading from somewhere else. Safe Surfing Sentinel blocks the ones with a track record of abuse, automatically.


Warns you before you walk into trouble. 

Phishing pages, fake "your download is ready" scams, lookalike domains designed to fool you at a glance — Sentinel checks the site you're about to visit against known patterns and abused domains, and steps in with a clear warning before you hand over anything you'll regret.


Catches pages trying to trap you. 

Fake "your account is locked" overlays, keyboard-lock tricks that won't let you leave, fulls-creen scare-ware — the kind of page designed to panic you into calling a scam number or installing something nasty. Sentinel spots the pattern and gives you your browser back.


Catches skimmers trying to steal your card of banking data.

Fake tracking-pixel overlays and payment forms that don't belong to the site — the Magecart-style trick behind real-world card-skimming scams — plus password fields quietly rigged to submit somewhere else. Sentinel watches closely on banking and payment pages and steps in before anything gets sent.


One whitelist. That's it. 

If Sentinel's being overly cautious on a site you trust, add it to the whitelist and it leaves that site alone. That's the entire configuration surface — because you shouldn't need a manual to feel safe online.



WHAT IT DOES NOT

It doesn't watch you. No browsing history is kept, no analytics, no "phoning home." Everything it checks, it checks locally, on your own device, against lists built into the extension itself.
It doesn't nag you. No up-sells, no "upgrade to premium," no account to create. It's free, it's open source, and it stays out of your way until it actually needs to warn you about something.

WHO THIS IS FOR

Anyone who wants real protection from the sketchier corners of the web without becoming their own IT department to get it working. Anyone who's ever clicked a link a half-second before thinking "wait, was that a good idea?"
