## Full description

**Zero configuration extension which warns users for SCAMS, SNOOPING
and SKIMMING**

Safe Surfing Sentinel is zero configuration — meaning no settings
pages full of toggles and switches you don't understand, no decisions
to get wrong. Install it, and it quietly gets to work.

**WHAT IT DOES**

🛡️ **Blocks the bad third parties.** A huge amount of what makes the
web slow, creepy, or dangerous isn't the site you're on — it's the
trackers and scripts that site is quietly loading from somewhere else.
Safe Surfing Sentinel blocks the ones with a track record of abuse,
automatically.

👀 **Warns you before you walk into trouble.** Phishing pages, fake
"your download is ready" scams, lookalike domains designed to fool you
at a glance — Sentinel checks the site you're about to visit against
known patterns and abused domains, and steps in with a clear warning
before you hand over anything you'll regret.

💳 **Catches skimmers trying to steal your card.** Fake tracking-pixel
overlays and payment forms that don't belong to the site — the
Magecart-style trick behind real-world card-skimming scams — plus
password fields quietly rigged to submit somewhere else. Sentinel
watches closely on banking and payment pages and steps in before
anything gets sent.

🔒 **Catches pages trying to trap you.** Fake "your account is locked"
overlays, keyboard-lock tricks that won't let you leave, fullscreen
scareware — the kind of page designed to panic you into calling a
scam number or installing something nasty. Sentinel spots the pattern
and gives you your browser back.

⚪ **One whitelist. That's it.** If Sentinel's being overly cautious on
a site you trust, add it to the whitelist and it leaves that site
alone. That's the entire configuration surface — because you shouldn't
need a manual to feel safe online.

**What it doesn't do:**

🚫 It doesn't watch you. No browsing history is kept, no analytics, no
"phoning home." Everything it checks, it checks locally, on your own
device, against lists built into the extension itself.

🚫 It doesn't nag you. No upsells, no "upgrade to premium," no account
to create. It's free, it's open source, and it stays out of your way
until it actually needs to warn you about something.

**Who it's for:** anyone who wants real protection from the sketchier
corners of the web without becoming their own IT department to get it —
parents, professionals, or anyone who's ever clicked a link a half-second
before thinking "wait, was that a good idea?"

Open source, actively maintained, and built by someone who's been
writing defensive, security-focused software for a long time. See the
source, the full privacy policy, and every third-party component it's
built on at the project's GitHub page.
